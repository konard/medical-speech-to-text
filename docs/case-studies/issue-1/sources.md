# Primary source register

Reviewed **2026-10-30**. Links below are the primary documentation, model cards,
papers and manufacturer pages used for the plan. Product capabilities, regions,
account eligibility, licenses and pricing can change; recheck at procurement and
each deployment. Findings are paraphrases, not independent vendor benchmarks.
Public documentation does not prove this project has paid access. Immutable local
execution evidence and dataset hashes are recorded separately in `data/`.

## Australian governance

- [OAIC APP 8 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information):
  cross-border disclosure/accountability assessment, not a universal rule that
  all health data must stay in Australia. This project's AU-only constraint is
  stricter and must be enforced as an explicit contract.
- [OAIC health research guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/health-service-providers/guide-to-health-privacy/chapter-9-research):
  consent and applicable research pathways require review; research intent is
  not blanket permission to send recordings to a vendor.
- [TGA AI/device software](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/manufacturing/artificial-intelligence-ai-and-medical-device-software),
  [clinical decision support guidance](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/understanding-clinical-decision-support-system-software-regulation):
  intended use and functions determine applicable assessment. Do not assert
  exemption or approval merely because the first version transcribes speech.

No current prices, supplier stock, universal accuracy ranking or clinical approval
are inferred from these sources. Unexecuted access, AU GPU quota, private dataset
collection and clinical validation remain prerequisites of the proposed study.
