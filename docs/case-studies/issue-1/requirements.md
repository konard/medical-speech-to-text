# Requirement and acceptance inventory

Source: the complete [issue body](data/issue.json), retrieved 2026-10-30. Issue
comments, PR conversation comments, inline review comments and reviews were read;
all were empty. The issue sub-issue endpoint returned an empty list. References to
other repositories are research inputs, not required sub-issues to close here.

“Plan” below means a complete executable research protocol, including prerequisites
and go/no-go criteria. “Built” means working code. “Observed” requires a recorded
execution; a vendor's claim or test double is not an observation of model quality.

The browser demo is a research instrument. It is not the production AWS service,
a complete CRM adapter, an Australian clinical validation, or evidence of universal
subsecond latency. Those are defined research/deployment stages; no backend keys,
cloud resources or real patient data were provided to execute them in this PR.
