# Dated primary-source register

Retrieved/reviewed **2026-10-30**. These sources support options and constraints;
vendor capability claims do not constitute measured medical accuracy or physical
phone validation. Existing engine, Australian architecture, audio, dataset and
hardware citations are audited in the [issue 1 register](../issue-1/sources.md).

The research selects a small tested browser baseline and preserves the full
20-family comparison. “All available options” means coverage of relevant runtime,
model, deployment and device classes with selection gates; it cannot certify every
unreleased model, proprietary checkpoint, browser/OS combination or private
account entitlement. Unknowns remain explicit research inputs rather than passes.

[Enable vulnerability alerts REST API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/repos/repos#enable-vulnerability-alerts): explicitly enables dependency alerts **and** the graph; used successfully to resolve the main prerequisite.
